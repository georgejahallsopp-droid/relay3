RELAY - send files between your devices over your own Wi-Fi

FIRST TIME
1. Double-click build.bat. It creates Relay.exe in this folder.
2. Double-click Relay.exe. A black window opens and your browser shows Relay.
3. If Windows asks whether to allow Relay on networks, tick "Private networks"
   and click Allow. Without this, your other devices can't connect.
4. Create an account in the browser.

EVERY TIME
- Start Relay.exe on this PC and leave its window open.
- On your phone or other computers (same Wi-Fi), open the address shown in the
  Relay window, e.g. http://192.168.1.20:8000, and sign in.
- Drag files in, or use Choose files. They appear on every signed-in device.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Uploaded files are stored in the RelayData folder next to Relay.exe.
  To move Relay to another PC, copy this whole folder.
- Forgot a password? Type reset in the Relay window.
- Type users in the Relay window to see all accounts.
- If phones can't connect: check they're on the same Wi-Fi, and that this PC's
  Wi-Fi is set to "Private" in Windows network settings.
- Traffic isn't encrypted, so only use Relay on networks you trust, like home Wi-Fi.
